## 2. Document Descriptions and Core Theses

### DOC-001: `Product Signal Intelligence OS_1.docx`
- **Scope**: Core architectural manifesto and system design notes for product intelligence.
- **Key Concepts**: Introduces the physical-product arbitrage concept, fastener domain focus, and the foundational idea of decoupling market signals from automated execution.
- **Initial Thesis**: Evaluates industrial fasteners as a high-density, low-spoilage category suitable for algorithmic capital allocation.

### DOC-002: `002.docx` (Product Specification & Engineering Standards)
- **Scope**: Mechanical and metallurgical specification for SS304 DIN 3017 worm-drive hose clamps.
- **Key Parameters**: Band widths (9mm vs 12mm), band thickness (0.6mm), housing design, screw head drive types, tightening torque ratings (DIN 3017 standards), W4 / SS304 material grades, corrosion resistance, and single-unit vs pack weight profiles.

### DOC-003: `003.docx` (Manufacturing Clusters & Supplier Research)
- **Scope**: Upstream supplier ecosystem analysis across Indian manufacturing hubs.
- **Key Clusters**: Rajkot and Jamnagar (Gujarat) precision fastener manufacturing ecosystems.
- **Observations**: Indicative factory-gate pricing tiers, minimum order quantities (MOQs: 1,000 to 10,000 units), trade terms, packaging options, and payment structures.

### DOC-004: `004.docx` (Marketplace Economics & Fee Schedules)
- **Scope**: Downstream sales channel economics on Amazon India.
- **Key Slabs**: Category referral fees (8.5%), closing fee tiers, FBA pick/pack fees, storage fees, weight-handling fee cliff structures (500g initial slab, ₹24 per additional 500g), and statutory GST on marketplace service commissions.

### DOC-005: `005.docx` (Competitor Teardown & Review Analytics)
- **Scope**: Competitive landscape analysis of existing Amazon India clamp listings.
- **Observations**: Listing structures, pricing strategies (pack-of-5, pack-of-10, pack-of-20), review score distributions, negative feedback root causes (stripping screws, rust on non-304 bands), and Buy Box ownership dynamics.

### DOC-006: `006.docx` (Taxation, Compliance & Statutory Tariffs)
- **Scope**: Statutory framework for inter-state and intra-state commercial distribution in India.
- **Key Elements**: Harmonized System Nomenclature (HSN 7326 / 8205), Goods and Services Tax (GST 18%), Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility rules, state e-way bill thresholds, and invoicing compliance.

### DOC-007: `007.docx` (Risk Modeling & Capital Runway)
- **Scope**: Adverse scenario analysis and capital sensitivity modeling.
- **Key Scenarios**: Supplier delivery delays, Amazon inventory reimbursement disputes, seasonal demand fluctuations, price war margin compression, return rate escalations, and working capital lockup duration.

### DOC-008: `008.docx` (Packaging, Freight & Volumetric Optimization)
- **Scope**: Inbound logistics and packaging geometry optimization.
- **Observations**: Individual polybagging vs boxed multi-packs, carton dimensions, volumetric freight penalty thresholds, transport damage rates, and courier slab boundaries.

### DOC-009: `009.docx` (Commercial Synthesis & Pilot Capital Allocation)
- **Scope**: Synthesis of physical, commercial, and financial inputs into the initial SS304 pilot strategy.
- **Key Recommendations**: Recommends initiating the proof-of-concept arbitrage experiment on specific high-velocity DIN 3017 clamp size variants (e.g. 16-25mm, 20-32mm), outlining pilot capital deployment budgets.
